Research Interests

Thermal behavior governs how materials transform, function and respond to their environment, underpinning processes ranging from phase transitions and molecular dynamics to catalysis and energy conversion. Yet, at the nanoscale, thermal behavior can differ markedly between individual particles and from bulk materials, making ensemble measurements insufficient to reveal intrinsic material properties.

Our research develops optical microscopy-based methods for quantitative measurement at the single-particle level. By combining precise temperature control with high-resolution optical imaging, we image dynamic thermal transitions and molecular adsorption processes in individual micro- and nanomaterials. These measurements reveal particle-to-particle heterogeneity and enable one-to-one correlation between material behavior and particle structure, providing a bottom-up approach to uncover structure–property relationships beyond ensemble measurements.
